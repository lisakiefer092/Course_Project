# The Environmental Impact of our Food
### The global food system is responsible for about 30% of greenhouse gas emissions, occupies 40% of the habitable land, and uses at least 70% of the freshwater available. However, different food items have different environmental impacts, and this dashboards aims to show this in detail for various exmaples. Users can find out which food items requrie the most resources and can therefore be mindful in their food consumption to minimize negative consequences.
## Data
#### The Data was pulled from the "Our World in Data" website. It is in three different CSV files, one considering the greenhouse gas emissions of food items, the other the water usage, and the third the land area used. The "environmental score" is achieved through a normalization of the data, giving within each data set each food item a score from 0-100, with 100 being given to the food item with the highest value.
## Dash Board 
#### The dahsboard consists of three tabs in which the data is visualized. In the second tab there is a horizontal bar graph showing the different environmental categories, with each category color-coded to be more visually appealing.
